    OPERATION BLACK TIDE

    Restricted

    Situation

    A Hegemony siege group has entered the Corvane system to bombard the relay at Corvane IV. You command the dreadnought CSV Halberd, flagship of Task Group 9. Your group will jump in at the system's edge and engage before the siege dreadnought reaches firing range of the relay.

    Mission

    Destroy the siege dreadnought VARR TOLUN and the fleet carrier ULTHANE. Keep Halberd in the fight. Expect enemy bombers, missile salvos and reinforcements.

    Commander's notes

    Your ship turns slowly. Plan your heading before you need it. Rotate fresh shield sectors toward the heaviest fire, use broadsides when your flank faces the enemy, and use the lance only when the bow is on target.

    How it fights

    Turrets track the reticle independently of the hull. A green SOLUTION ring appears when your reticle sits on the lead marker of the locked target; gunnery then corrects the aim to the exact intercept. Broadsides only bear when your flank faces the target. The lance needs the bow within four degrees. Batteries set to AUTO pick their own targets using the fire doctrine.

    Shields are split into four sectors. Reinforcing one sector raises its capacity and regeneration and draws energy from the others.
