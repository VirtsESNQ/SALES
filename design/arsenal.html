Rules of thumb

Turrets (turbolaser, secondary, ion) swing on their own within the dorsal arc. Broadsides only fire across the flank, so turn your side to the target. The spinal lance fires only within 4° of the bow. The plasma cannons, mass drivers and torpedoes need the bow roughly on target. Point defence and flak fire automatically. Missiles and torpedoes need a locked target. Mines drop astern.
