VARR TOLUN

Varr-class siege dreadnought
Port elevation · bow right
Armament
    How to beat it

    The siege cannon glows orange at the bow before it fires. Turn your ship out of its line or move shields to the front. Its stern castle is the weak spot: the engines sit under thin armour.

    Model notes

    Gothic "battle cathedral". Gold-clad ram prow with the siege-cannon emitter at the tip. Two gun decks, 24 broadside ports in total: 12 per flank, 6 on each deck.. Spires and pinnacles along the spine. Stern castle crowned by a great spire. Engine glow is orange-red.
